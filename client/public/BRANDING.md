# Logo HCMUT

`hcmut-logo.png` is the unchanged 3509 × 2481 image supplied by the repository owner on 2026-11-01, with the white center and border.

`hcmut-mark.svg` embeds that PNG. Its viewBox frames the logo and an outline clip hides the surrounding black canvas, without redrawing the lettering or colored facets. It is shared by BKLogo and the favicon and served locally.

User-provided reference: https://share.google/rf9XT6NiSEVohoEGi (the supplied attachment was used because the share link could not be resolved).
